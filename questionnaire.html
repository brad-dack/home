Section 8 of 9

AML Governance Roles

For most small real estate agencies, it is reasonable for one person to hold multiple AML/CTF governance roles. Where a business has one or two principals, consolidating governance roles is generally appropriate and proportionate.

8.1 — Governing Body

Responsible for overall governance and executive-level decisions. Responsibilities include:
  • Setting the strategic direction of the AML/CTF Program
  • Appointing AML/CTF roles
  • Reviewing compliance reporting
  • Ensuring sufficient resources
In smaller businesses, this is commonly the business owner.

8.2 — Senior Manager

Responsible for approving the AML/CTF Program and making decisions about high-risk customers. Responsibilities include:
  • Approving AML/CTF policies and updates
  • Deciding whether to continue acting for high-risk customers
  • Overseeing AML/CTF decision-making
Commonly the licensee in charge or another senior decision-maker.

8.3 — AML/CTF Compliance Officer

Oversees day-to-day AML/CTF compliance. Must be an Australian resident in a management-level role. Responsibilities include:
  • Overseeing AML/CTF compliance
  • Monitoring adherence to procedures
  • Managing escalated or high-risk matters
Commonly the licensee in charge, business owner or senior operational staff member.

8.4 — Other AML/CTF Personnel

Other staff who may support AML/CTF obligations as part of their normal duties — e.g. sales agents, office managers, trust account personnel.

About this section. The AML/CTF Act requires every reporting entity to have specific people responsible for overseeing compliance. This section is about identifying who in your business is best placed to fill those roles.

For most small real estate agencies, one person can hold more than one role. The answers you provide here are a starting point only and will be discussed and confirmed with you before your program is finalised. There are no wrong answers.

8.1 Governing Body

Responsible for overall governance and executive-level decisions. Responsibilities include setting the strategic direction of the AML/CTF Program, appointing AML/CTF roles, reviewing compliance reporting, and ensuring sufficient resources are available. In smaller businesses, this is commonly the business owner.

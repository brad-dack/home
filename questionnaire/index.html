7.1 Governing Body

Responsible for overall governance and executive-level decisions. Responsibilities include setting the strategic direction of the AML/CTF Program, appointing AML/CTF roles, reviewing compliance reporting, and ensuring sufficient resources are available. In smaller businesses, this is commonly the business owner.
